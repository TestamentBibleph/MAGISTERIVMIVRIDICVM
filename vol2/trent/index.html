Council of Trent

Concilium Tridentinum

1545–1563

Editorial note

Primary dogmatic contributions: Scripture and Tradition; original sin; justification (the most theologically elaborate single decree of any Latin ecumenical council); sacraments; Eucharist and transubstantiation restated with full precision; orders and matrimony; purgatory, indulgences, images, and relics (Session 25 — primary Latin ecumenical reception of Nicaea II).

Trent's decree on justification (Decretum de iustificatione, Session 6) is the longest and most technically precise soteriological definition in the Latin conciliar tradition and should be treated as the doctrinal center of the Trent chapter.

This council is presented in parts for faster reading.

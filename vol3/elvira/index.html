CANON LXXIX. Concerning those who play at dice.
CANON LXXX. Concerning freedmen.
CANON LXXXI. Concerning letters of women.
Cum convenissent sancti et religiosi episcopi in ecclesia Elibertina, hoc est, Felix episcopus Aequitanus, Conscius episcopus Cordobensis, Savinus episcopus Spalensis, Camerinus episcopus Tacitanus, Sinagius episcopus Pegrensis, Secundusdinus episcopus Catalensis, Pardus episcopus Memtessanus, Flavianus episcopus Eliberritanus, Catonius episcopus Corsitanus, Liberius episcopus Meritensis, Valerius episcopus Caesaragustanus, Decentius episcopus Legionensis, Melantius episcopus Toletanus, Ianuarius episcopus de Fiblaria, Vincentius episcopus Ossonobensis, Quintianus episcopus Elborensis, Successus episcopus de Elicroca, Euticianus episcopus Bastitanus, Patricius episcopus Malacitanus, residentibus etiam triginta sex presbyteris adstantibus diaconibus et omni plebe episcopi universi dixerunt:
The holy and religious bishops convened in the church of Elvira, namely: Felix, bishop of Acci; Conscius, bishop of Cordoba; Sabinus, bishop of Seville; Camerinus, bishop of Tacita; Sinagius, bishop of Pégren; Secundinus, bishop of Catala; Pardus, bishop of Mentesa; Flavianus, bishop of Elvira; Catonius, bishop of Corts; Liberius, bishop of Mérida; Valerius, bishop of Zaragoza; Decentius, bishop of León; Melantius, bishop of Toledo; Januarius, bishop of Fiblaria; Vincentius, bishop of Ossonoba; Quintianus, bishop of Elbora; Successus, bishop of Elicroca; Euticianus, bishop of Basti; Patricius, bishop of Málaga; thirty-six presbyters also residing, deacons standing by, and all the people. The bishops all said:
¶2
CANON I
CONCERNING THOSE WHO AFTER BAPTISM SACRIFICE TO IDOLS
Placuit, ut quicumque post fidem baptismi salutaris adulta aetate ad templum idoli idolatriis accesserit et fecerit, quod est crimen principale, nec in finem eum ad communionem suscipere.
It pleased that whoever after the faith of saving baptism, being of adult age, shall have approached the temple of an idol for idolatries and done that which is the principal crime, he shall not be received to communion even at the end.
¶3
CANON II
CONCERNING PRIESTS OF THE GENTILES WHO AFTER BAPTISM HAVE SACRIFICED
Flamines, qui post fidem lavacri et regenerationis sacrificaverint, eo quod geminaverint scelera accedente homicidio vel triplicaverint facinus cohaerente moechia, placuit eos nec in finem accipere communionem.
Flamens who after the faith of the laver and regeneration have sacrificed, because they have doubled their crimes, homicide being added, or have tripled their crime, adultery cohering, it pleased that they not receive communion even at the end.
¶4
CANON III
CONCERNING THE SAME, IF THEY HAVE GIVEN ONLY A GIFT TO IDOLS
Item flamines, qui non immolaverunt, sed munus tantum dederunt, eo quod se a funestis abstinuerunt sacrificiis, placuit in finem eis praestare communionem, acta tamen legitima poenitentia. Item ipsi, si post poenitentiam fuerint moechati, placuit ulterius eis non esse dandam communionem, ne lusisse de dominica communione videatur.
Likewise, flamens who have not sacrificed but have given only a gift, because they have abstained from the deadly sacrifices, it pleased that communion be granted to them at the end, legitimate penance having been performed. Likewise, if after penance they have committed adultery, it pleased that communion not be given to them further, lest it seem that they have trifled with the Lord's communion.
¶5
CANON IV
CONCERNING THE SAME, IF BEING STILL CATECHUMENS THEY SACRIFICE, WHEN THEY SHOULD BE BAPTIZED
Item flamines, si fuerint caticumini et se a sacrificiis abstinuerint, post triennii tempora placuit ad baptismum admitti debere.
Likewise, flamens if they shall have been catechumens and have abstained themselves from sacrifices, after a space of three years it pleased that they be admitted to baptism.
¶6
CANON V
IF A MISTRESS THROUGH JEALOUSY HAS KILLED A HANDMAID
Si qua femina furore zeli accensa flagellis verberaverit ancillam suam, ita ut intra tertium diem animam cum cruciatu effundat, eo quod incertum sit, voluntate an casu occiderit, si voluntate, post septem annos, si casu, per quinque annorum tempora, acta legitima poenitentia ad communionem placuit admitti. Quod si infra tempora constituta fuerit infirmata, accipiat communionem.
If any woman, inflamed with the fury of jealousy, shall have beaten her handmaid with scourges, so that within the third day she pours forth her soul with torment, because it is uncertain whether she killed her willingly or by accident; if willingly, after seven years; if by accident, after a period of five years, legitimate penance having been performed, it pleased that she be admitted to communion. But if within the established time she shall have become infirm, let her receive communion.
¶7
CANON VI
IF ANYONE THROUGH SORCERY HAS KILLED A MAN
Si quis vero maleficio interficiat alterum, eo quod sine idolatria perficere scelus non potuit, nec in finem impertiendam esse illi communionem.
If anyone by sorcery kills another, because he could not accomplish the crime without idolatry, it pleased that communion not be granted to him even at the end.
¶8
CANON VII
CONCERNING PENITENTS OF ADULTERY, IF THEY HAVE SINNED AGAIN
Si quis forte fidelis post lapsum moechiae post tempora constituta acta poenitentia denuo fuerit fornicatus, placuit eum nec in finem habere communionem.
If any faithful person, after the lapse of adultery and after the established times, penance having been performed, shall have committed fornication again, it pleased that he not have communion even at the end.
¶9
CANON VIII
CONCERNING WOMEN WHO, HAVING LEFT THEIR HUSBANDS, MARRY OTHERS
Item feminae, quae nulla praecedente causa reliquerint viros suos et alteris se copulaverint, nec in finem accipiant communionem.
Likewise, women who, with no preceding cause, have left their husbands and have joined themselves to others, let them not receive communion even at the end.
¶10
CANON IX
CONCERNING WOMEN WHO LEAVE ADULTEROUS HUSBANDS AND MARRY OTHERS
Item fidelis femina, quae adulterum maritum reliquerit fidelem et alterum ducit, prohibeatur ne ducat. Si autem duxerit, non prius accipiat communionem, quam is, quem relinquit, de saeculo exierit, nisi forte necessitas infirmitatis dare compulerit.
Likewise, a faithful woman who shall have left an adulterous faithful husband and marries another, let her be forbidden to marry. But if she shall have married, let her not receive communion until he whom she has left shall have departed from the world, unless perhaps the necessity of infirmity compels that it be given.
¶11
CANON X
CONCERNING A FORSAKEN CATECHUMEN WOMAN, IF SHE HAS TAKEN ANOTHER
Si ea, quam caticuminus relinquit, duxerit maritum, potest ad fontem lavacri admitti. Hoc et circa feminas caticuminas erit observandum. Quod si fuerit, quae ducitur ab eo, qui uxorem inculpatam relinquit, et cum scierit illum ab uxore, quam sine causa relinquit, placuit in finem huiusmodi dare communionem.
If she whom a catechumen leaves has married a husband, she can be admitted to the font of the laver. This shall also be observed concerning catechumen women. But if she who is taken by him who leaves a blameless wife, and she shall have known that he leaves his wife without cause, it pleased that such a woman receive communion at the end.
¶12
CANON XI
CONCERNING A CATECHUMEN, IF HE HAS BECOME GRAVELY ILL
Intra quinquennii autem tempora caticumina, si graviter fuerit infirmata, addendum ei baptismum placuit non denegari.
If a catechumen within the space of five years shall have been gravely ill, it pleased that baptism not be denied to him.
¶13
CANON XII
CONCERNING WOMEN WHO HAVE PRACTICED PIMPING
Mater vel parens vel quaelibet fidelis, si lenocinium exercuerit, eo quod alienum vendiderit corpus vel potius suum, placuit eam nec in fine accipere communionem.
A mother or parent or any faithful woman, if she shall have practiced pimping, because she has sold the body of another or rather her own, it pleased that she not receive communion even at the end.
¶14
CANON XIII
CONCERNING VIRGINS CONSECRATED TO GOD, IF THEY HAVE COMMITTED ADULTERY
Virgines, quae se deo dicaverunt, si pactum perdiderint virginitatis atque eidem libidini servierint non intellegentes, quod admiserint, placuit nec in fine dandam eis esse communionem. Quod si semet persuaserint, quod infirmitate corporis lapsae fuerint, etiam tempore vitae suae huiusmodi feminae egerint poenitentiam et abstinuerint se a coitu, eo quod lapsae potius videantur, placuit eas in finem communionem accipere debere.
Virgins who have consecrated themselves to God, if they shall have lost the compact of virginity and served the same lust, not understanding what they have admitted, it pleased that communion not be given to them even at the end. But if they shall have persuaded themselves that they were lapsed on account of infirmity of the body, and such women shall have done penance during the time of their life and abstained themselves from intercourse, because they seem rather to have been lapsed, it pleased that they ought to receive communion at the end.
¶15
CANON XIV
CONCERNING SECULAR VIRGINS, IF THEY HAVE COMMITTED FORNICATION
Virgines, quae virginitatem suam non custodierint, si eosdem, qui eas violaverint, duxerint et tenuerint maritos, eo quod solas nuptias violaverint, post annum sine poenitentia reconciliari debebunt. vel si alios cognoverint viros, eo quod moechatae sint, placuit per quinquennii tempora acta legitima poenitentia admitti eas ad communionem.
Virgins who have not kept their virginity, if they shall have taken and kept as husbands those who violated them, because they have violated only the marriage, after one year they ought to be reconciled without penance. Or if they shall have known other men, because they have committed adultery, it pleased that after a space of five years, legitimate penance having been performed, they be admitted to communion.
¶16
CANON XV
CONCERNING THE MARRIAGE OF THOSE WHO COME FROM PAGANISM
Propter copiam puellarum gentilibus minime in matrimonium dandae sunt virgines christianae, ne aetas in flore tumens in adulterio animae resolvatur.
On account of the abundance of girls, Christian virgins are by no means to be given in marriage to gentiles, lest the age swelling in flower be dissolved into adultery of the soul.
¶17
CANON XVI
CONCERNING FAITHFUL GIRLS, THAT THEY NOT BE JOINED TO UNBELIEVERS
Hereticis, qui errant ab ecclesia catholica, nec ipsis catholicas esse dandas puellas, sed neque Iudaeis neque hereticis dare placuit, eo quod nulla possit esse societas fideli cum infidele. Si contra interdictum fecerint parentes, abstinere per quinquennium placet.
To heretics who err from the catholic Church, neither are catholic girls to be given, but neither to Jews nor to heretics is it pleasing to give, because there can be no society between a faithful and an unbeliever. If parents shall have acted against this interdict, it pleases that they abstain for five years.
¶18
CANON XVII
CONCERNING THOSE WHO JOIN THEIR DAUGHTERS TO PRIESTS OF THE GENTILES
Si quis forte sacerdotibus idolorum filias suas iunxerint, placuit nec in finem eis dandam esse communionem.
If anyone perhaps shall have joined his daughters to priests of idols, it pleased that communion not be given to them even at the end.
¶19
CANON XVIII
CONCERNING PRIESTS AND MINISTERS, IF THEY HAVE COMMITTED FORNICATION
Episcopi, presbyteri, diacones, si in ministerio positi detecti fuerint, quod sint moechati, placuit et propter scandalum et propter infandum crimen nec in finem eos communionem accipere debere.
Bishops, presbyters, deacons, if they have been detected that they have committed fornication, placed in the ministry, it pleased that on account of the scandal and on account of the unspeakable crime they ought not to receive communion even at the end.
¶20
CANON XIX
CONCERNING CLERICS PURSUING BUSINESS AND FAIRS
Episcopi, presbyteri et diaconi de locis suis negotiandi causa non discedant, nec circumeuntes provincias quaestuosas nundinas sectentur. Sane ad victum sibi conquirendum aut filium aut libertum aut mercenarium aut amicum aut quemlibet mittant et, si voluerint negotiari, intra provinciam negotientur.
Bishops, presbyters, and deacons shall not leave their places for the sake of trading, nor, going about the provinces, pursue gainful fairs. But let them send for acquiring their living either a son or a freedman or a hireling or a friend or any, and if they wish to trade, let them trade within the province.
¶21
CANON XX
CONCERNING CLERIC AND LAY USURERS
Si quis clericorum deditus fuerit usuras accipere, placuit degradari et abstineri. Si quis etiam laicus accepisse probatur usuras et promiserit correptus iam cessaturum nec ulterius exacturum, placet ei veniam tribui. Si vero in ea iniquitate duraverit, ab ecclesia sciat se esse proiciendum.
If any cleric shall have been given to receiving usury, it pleased that he be degraded and abstained. If any layman also is proved to have received usury and, having been corrected, shall have promised that he will now cease and not exact further, it pleases that pardon be granted to him. But if he shall have persevered in that iniquity, let him know that he is to be cast out from the church.
¶22
CANON XXI
CONCERNING THOSE WHO COME TO CHURCH TOO LATE
Si quis in civitate positus tres dominicas ad ecclesiam non accesserit, pauco tempore abstineatur, ut correptus esse videatur.
If anyone placed in the city shall not have come to church for three Sundays, let him be abstained for a short time, that he may seem to have been corrected.
¶23
CANON XXII
CONCERNING CATHOLICS PASSING OVER INTO HERESY, IF THEY RETURN
Si quis de catholica ecclesia ad heresim transitum fecerit rursusque ad ecclesiam recurrerit, placuit huic poenitentiam non esse denegandam, eo quod cognoverit peccatum suum. Qui etiam decem annis agat poenitentiam, cui post decem annis praestari communio debet. Si vero infantes fuerint transducti, quod non suo vitio peccaverunt, incunctanter recipi debeant.
If anyone from the catholic church shall have made a passage into heresy and shall have returned again to the church, it pleased that penance not be denied to him, because he has acknowledged his sin. He shall also do penance for ten years, to whom after ten years communion ought to be granted. But if infants have been led over, who sinned not by their own fault, let them be received without hesitation.
¶24
CANON XXIII
CONCERNING THE TIMES OF FASTS
Ieiunii superpositiones per singulos menses placuit celebrari exceptis diebus duorum mensium Iulio et Augusto ob eorundem infirmitatem.
It pleased that the superpositions of fasts be celebrated each month, excepting the days of two months, July and August, on account of the infirmity of those months.
¶25
CANON XXIV
CONCERNING THOSE WHO ARE BAPTIZED ABROAD, THAT THEY NOT COME TO THE CLERGY
Omnes, qui in peregre fuerint baptizati, eo quod eorum minime sit cognita vita, placuit ad clerum non esse promovendos in alienis provinciis.
All who have been baptized abroad, because their life is by no means known, it pleased that they not be promoted to the clergy in foreign provinces.
¶26
CANON XXV
CONCERNING LETTERS OF COMMUNION OF CONFESSORS
Omnis, qui attulerit litteras confessorias sublato nomine confessoris, eo quod omnes sub hac nominis gloria partim concutiant simplices, communicatoriae ei dandae sunt litterae.
Anyone who shall have brought confessorial letters, the name of the confessor having been suppressed, because under the glory of this name some partly shake the simple, letters of communion are to be given to him.
¶27
CANON XXVI
CONCERNING THIS, THAT WE FAST ON EVERY SABBATH
Errorem placuit corrigi, ut omni sabbati die superpositionem celebremus.
It pleased that the error be corrected, that we celebrate the superposition on every day of the Sabbath.
¶28
CANON XXVII
CONCERNING CLERICS, THAT THEY HAVE NO STRANGE WOMAN IN THE HOUSE
Episcopus vel quilibet alius clericus aut sororem aut filiam virginem dicatam deo tantum secum habeat, extraneam vero nequaquam habere placuit.
A bishop or any other cleric shall have only a sister or a daughter, a virgin consecrated to God, with him; but it pleased that he have no strange woman.
¶29
CANON XXVIII
CONCERNING THE OFFERINGS OF THOSE WHO DO NOT COMMUNICATE
Episcopum placuit ab eo, qui non communicat, munera accipere non debere.
It pleased that a bishop ought not to receive gifts from him who does not communicate.
¶30
CANON XXIX
CONCERNING THE ENERGUMENS, HOW THEY ARE TO BE HAD IN THE CHURCH
Inerguminum, qui ab erratico spiritu exagitatur, huius nomen neque ad altare cum oblatione esse recitandum, neque permittendum, ut sua manu in ecclesia ministret.
It pleased that the name of an energumen, who is agitated by a wandering spirit, not be recited at the altar with the oblation, nor be permitted to minister in the church with his own hand.
¶31
CANON XXX
CONCERNING THOSE WHO AFTER THE LAVER HAVE COMMITTED FORNICATION, THAT THEY NOT BECOME SUBDEACONS
Subdiaconem eum ordinari non debere, qui in adolescentia sua fuerit moechatus, eo quod postmodum per subreptionem ad altiorem gradum non sit promovendus. Si autem aliqui sunt in praeteritum ordinati, amoveantur.
It pleased that he who in his youth has committed fornication ought not to be ordained a subdeacon, because afterwards by surreption he is not to be promoted to a higher degree. But if any have been ordained in the past, let them be removed.
¶32
CANON XXXI
CONCERNING YOUTHS WHO AFTER THE LAVER OF SAVING FAITH HAVE COMMITTED FORNICATION
Adolescentes, qui post fidem lavacri salutaris fuerint moechati, cum duxerint uxores, acta legitima poenitentia placuit ad communionem eos admitti.
Youths who after the faith of the saving laver have committed fornication, when they shall have taken wives, legitimate penance having been performed, it pleased that they be admitted to communion.
¶33
CANON XXXII
CONCERNING EXCOMMUNICATED PRESBYTERS, THAT IN NECESSITY THEY GIVE COMMUNION
Si quis gravi lapsu in ruinam mortis inciderit, placuit agere poenitentiam non debere sine episcopi consultu, sed potius apud episcopum agat, cogente tamen infirmitate nec est presbyterorum aut diaconorum communionem talibus praestare debere, nisi eis iusserit episcopus.
If anyone has fallen into a grave lapse into ruin of death, it pleased that he ought not to do penance without the counsel of the bishop, but rather let him do it before the bishop; nevertheless, with infirmity compelling, it is not necessary for presbyters or deacons to give communion to such unless the bishop shall have commanded them.
¶34
CANON XXXIII
CONCERNING BISHOPS AND MINISTERS, THAT THEY ABSTAIN FROM THEIR WIVES
Placuit in totum prohibere episcopis, presbyteris et diaconibus ac subdiaconibus positis in ministerio abstinere se a coniugibus suis et non generare filios. Quod quicumque fecerit, ab honore clericatus exterminetur.
It pleased that bishops, presbyters, and deacons, placed in the ministry, be altogether forbidden to abstain from their wives and not beget children. Whoever shall have done this, let him be cast out from the honor of the clericate.
¶35
CANON XXXIV
THAT CANDLES NOT BE LIT IN CEMETERIES
Cereos per diem placuit in cimiterio non incendi, inquietandi enim sanctorum spiritus non sunt. Qui haec non observaverint, arceantur ab ecclesiae communione.
It pleased that candles not be lit in a cemetery by day, for the spirits of the saints are not to be disturbed. Those who shall not have observed these things, let them be kept away from the communion of the church.
¶36
CANON XXXV
THAT WOMEN NOT KEEP VIGIL IN CEMETERIES
Placuit prohiberi, ne feminae in cimiterio pervigilent, eo quod saepe sub obtentu orationis latenter scelera committant.
It pleased to be forbidden that women keep vigil in a cemetery, because often under pretext of prayer they secretly commit crimes.
¶37
CANON XXXVI
THAT PICTURES NOT BE IN THE CHURCH
Placuit picturas in ecclesia esse non debere nec, quod colitur aut adoratur, in parietibus depingatur.
It pleased that pictures not be in the church, nor that what is worshipped or adored be painted on the walls.
¶38
CANON XXXVII
CONCERNING ENERGUMENS NOT BAPTIZED
Eos, qui ab immundis spiritibus vexantur, si in fine mortis fuerint constituti, baptizari placet. Si vero fideles fuerint, dandam eis esse communionem. Prohibendum etiam, ne lucernas publice accendant. Si facere contra interdictum voluerint, abstineantur a communione.
It pleases that those who are vexed by unclean spirits, if they have been placed at the end of death, be baptized. But if they are faithful, that communion be given to them. It is also to be forbidden that they publicly light candles. If they wish to act contrary to the interdict, let them be abstained from communion.
¶39
CANON XXXVIII
THAT IN NECESSITY EVEN THE FAITHFUL MAY BAPTIZE
Peregre navigantes aut, si ecclesia in proximo non fuerit, posse fidelem, qui lavacrum suum integrum habet nec sit bigamus, baptizare in necessitate infirmitatis positum caticuminum, ita ut, si supervixerit, ad episcopum eum perducat, ut per manus impositionem perfici possit.
Those sailing abroad, or if the church is not near, a faithful person who has his laver whole and is not a bigamist can baptize a catechumen placed in necessity of infirmity, so that if he survives, he bring him to the bishop, that he may be perfected by the imposition of hands.
¶40
CANON XXXIX
CONCERNING GENTILES, IF IN DANGER THEY SEEK TO BE BAPTIZED
Gentiles, si in infirmitate desideraverint sibi manus imponi, si fuerit eorum ex aliqua parte honesta vita, placuit eis manum imponi et fieri christianos.
Gentiles, if in infirmity they have desired that hands be imposed on them, if their life has been in some part honest, it pleased that hands be imposed on them and that they become Christians.
¶41
CANON XL
THAT THE FAITHFUL NOT RECEIVE THAT WHICH IS OFFERED TO IDOLS
Prohibere placuit, ut, cum rationes suas accipiunt possessores, quicquid ad idolum datum fuerit, acceptum non ferant. Si vero post interdictum fecerint, per quinquennii spatia temporis a communione esse arcendos.
It pleased to be forbidden that when possessors receive their accounts, whatever has been given to an idol, they not accept as received. But if after the interdict they shall have done it, they are to be kept from communion for a space of five years.
¶42
CANON XLI
THAT MASTERS FORBID SLAVES TO WORSHIP IDOLS
Admonere placuit fideles, ut, quantum possunt, prohibeant, ne idola in domibus suis habeant, si vero vim metuunt servorum, vel seipsos puros conservent. Si non fecerint, alieni ab ecclesia habeantur.
It pleased to admonish the faithful that, as much as they can, they forbid idols to be in their houses; but if they fear the violence of slaves, let them keep themselves pure. If they shall not have done so, let them be held as aliens from the church.
¶43
CANON XLII
CONCERNING THOSE WHO COME TO THE FAITH, WHEN THEY ARE TO BE BAPTIZED
Eos, qui ad primam fidem credulitatis accedunt, si bonae fuerint conversationis, intra biennium temporis placuit ad baptismi gratiam admitti debere, nisi infirmitate compellente coegerit ratio vel socio subvenire periclitanti vel gratiam postulanti.
It pleased that those who come to the first faith of belief, if they have been of good conversation, be admitted to the grace of baptism within the space of two years, unless infirmity compelling, reason compels, or a partner in peril or one seeking grace.
¶44
CANON XLIII
CONCERNING THE CELEBRATION OF PENTECOST
Parvam institutionem emendari placuit iuxta auctoritatem scripturarum, ut cuncti diem pentecostes celebremus, quod, qui non fecerit, quasi novam heresem induxisse notetur.
It pleased that the small institution be corrected according to the authority of the Scriptures, that all celebrate the day of Pentecost; which if anyone shall not have done, let him be noted as having introduced a new heresy.
¶45
CANON XLIV
CONCERNING PAGAN PROSTITUTES, IF THEY CONVERT
Meretrix, quae aliquando fuerit, et postea habuerit maritum, si postmodum ad credulitatem venerit, incunctanter placuit esse recipiendam.
A prostitute who once existed and afterwards had a husband, if she shall afterward come to belief, it pleased that she be received without hesitation.
¶46
CANON XLV
CONCERNING CATECHUMENS WHO DO NOT FREQUENT THE CHURCH
Qui aliquando fuerit caticuminus et per infinita tempora numquam ad ecclesiam accesserit, si eum de clero quisquam agnoverit voluisse esse christianum aut testes aliqui exstiterint fideles, placuit ei baptismum non negari, eo quod in vetere homine deliquisse videatur.
Whoever has been a catechumen and through infinite times has never come to church, if anyone of the clergy shall have known that he wished to be a Christian, or some faithful witnesses shall have existed, it pleased that baptism not be denied to him, because he seems to have offended in the old man.
¶47
CANON XLVI
CONCERNING THE FAITHFUL, IF THEY HAVE APOSTATIZED, HOW LONG THEY DO PENANCE
Si quis fidelis apostata per infinita tempora ad ecclesiam non accesserit, si tamen aliquando fuerit reversus nec fuerit idolator, post decem annos placuit eum communionem accipere.
If any faithful person, an apostate, through infinite times has not come to church, if yet at any time he shall have returned and has not been an idolater, it pleased that after ten years he receive communion.
¶48
CANON XLVII
CONCERNING HIM WHO HAS A WIFE, IF HE COMMITS FORNICATION REPEATEDLY
Si quis fidelis habens uxorem non semel, sed saepe fuerit moechatus, in finem mortis est conveniendus. Quod si se promiserit cessaturum, detur ei communio. Si resuscitatus rursus fuerit moechatus, placuit ulterius non ludere eum de communione pacis.
If any faithful person having a wife, not once but often, has committed fornication, he is to be admonished at the end of death. But if he shall have promised that he will cease, let communion be given to him. If, having been restored, he shall have committed fornication again, it pleased that he not trifle further with the communion of peace.
¶49
CANON XLVIII
CONCERNING THOSE TO BE BAPTIZED, THAT CLERICS RECEIVE NOTHING
Emendari placuit, ut hi, qui baptizantur, ut fieri solebat, nummos in concam non mittant, ne sacerdos, quod gratis accepit, pretio distrahere videatur, neque pedes eorum lavandi sunt a sacerdotibus vel clericis.
It pleased to be corrected that those who are baptized, as was customary, do not put coins into the basin, lest the priest, what he has received gratis, seem to sell at a price; nor are their feet to be washed by priests or clerics.
¶50
CANON XLIX
CONCERNING THE FRUITS OF THE FAITHFUL, THAT THEY NOT BE BLESSED BY JEWS
Admonere placuit possessores, ut non patiantur fructus suos, quos a deo percipiunt, cum gratiarum actione a Iudaeis benedici, ne nostram irritam et infirmam faciant benedictionem. Si quis post interdictum facere usurpaverit, penitus ab ecclesia abiciatur.
It pleased to admonish the possessors that they not suffer their fruits, which they receive from God, to be blessed by Jews with thanksgiving, lest they make our blessing void and weak. If anyone after the interdict shall have usurped to do so, let him be altogether cast out from the church.
¶51
CANON L
CONCERNING CHRISTIANS WHO EAT WITH JEWS
Si vero aliquis clericus sive fidelis fuerit, qui cum Iudaeis cibum sumpserit, placuit eum a communione abstineri, ut debeat emendari.
If any cleric or faithful person shall have taken food with Jews, it pleased that he be abstained from communion, that he may be corrected.
¶52
CANON LI
CONCERNING HERETICS, THAT THEY NOT BE PROMOTED TO THE CLERGY
Ex omni heresi, si qui ad nos fidelis venerit, minime est ad clerum promovendus vel, si qui sunt in praeteritum ordinati, sine dubio deponantur.
From any heresy, if any faithful person comes to us, he is by no means to be promoted to the clergy; or if any have been ordained in the past, let them be deposed without doubt.
¶53
CANON LII
CONCERNING THOSE WHO PLACE INFAMOUS LIBELS IN THE CHURCH
Hi, qui inventi fuerint libellos famosos in ecclesia ponere, anathematizentur.
Those who have been found to place infamous libels in the church, let them be anathematized.
¶54
CANON LIII
CONCERNING BISHOPS WHO COMMUNICATE WITH ANOTHER'S EXCOMMUNICATED PERSON
Placuit cunctis, ut ab eo episcopo quis recipiat communionem, a quo abstentus in crimine aliquo quis fuerit. Quod si alius episcopus praesumpserit eum admitti illo adhuc minime faciente vel consentiente, a quo fuerat communione privatus, sciat se huiusmodi causas inter fratres esse cum status sui periculo praestaturum.
It pleased by all, that from that bishop from whom anyone has been abstained for some crime, from him let him receive communion. But if another bishop shall have presumed to admit him, the former not doing nor consenting, by whom he had been deprived of communion, let him know that he will be to answer for such causes among the brothers with the peril of his state.
¶55
CANON LIV
CONCERNING PARENTS WHO BREAK THE FAITH OF BETROTHAL
Si qui parentes fidem fregerint sponsaliorum, triennii tempore abstineant se a communione. Si tamen idem sponsus vel sponsa in gravi crimine fuerint depraehensi, excusati erunt parentes. Si vero in eodem fuerit vitio et polluerunt se consentiendo, superior sententia servetur.
If any parents break the faith of betrothal, let them abstain themselves from communion for the space of three years. Yet if the same betrothed man or betrothed woman shall have been detected in grave crime, the parents will be excused. But if it shall have been in the same vice and they have polluted themselves by consenting, let the former sentence be kept.
¶56
CANON LV
CONCERNING PRIESTS OF THE GENTILES WHO NO LONGER SACRIFICE
Sacerdotes, qui tantum coronam sacrificantes portant nec sacrificant nec de suis sumptibus aliquid idolis praestant, placuit post biennium accipere communionem.
Priests who only wear the crown, sacrificing, and do not sacrifice nor give anything of their expenses to idols, it pleased that they receive communion after two years.
¶57
CANON LVI
CONCERNING MAGISTRATES AND RULERS
Magistratus vero anno, quo agit duumviratum, prohibendum placet, ut se ab ecclesia cohibeat.
Magistrates, however, in the year in which they exercise the duumvirate, it pleased to be forbidden that he keep himself from the church.
¶58
CANON LVII
CONCERNING THOSE WHO HAVE GIVEN GARMENTS FOR ADORNING A PROCESSION
Matronae vel earum mariti vestimenta sua ad ornandum saeculariter pompam non dent, et si fecerint, triennii tempore abstineantur.
Matrons or their husbands shall not give their garments for adorning a procession secularly, and if they have done so, let them be abstained for the space of three years.
¶59
CANON LVIII
CONCERNING THOSE WHO CARRY LETTERS OF COMMUNION, THAT THEY BE QUESTIONED CONCERNING THE FAITH
Placuit ubicumque et maxime in eo loco, in quo primae cathedrae constitutus est episcopus, ut interrogentur hi, qui communicatorias litteras tradunt, an omnia recta habeant et suo testimonio comprobent.
It pleased that everywhere and especially in that place where a bishop of the first see is constituted, that those who hand over letters of communion be questioned whether they have all things right and prove by their own testimony.
¶60
CANON LIX
THAT THE FAITHFUL NOT GO UP TO THE CAPITOL FOR THE SAKE OF SACRIFICING
Prohibendum, ne quis christianus aut gentilis ad idolum capitolii causa sacrificandi ascendat et videat. Quod si fecerit, pari crimine teneatur. Si fuerit fidelis, post decem annos acta poenitentia recipiatur.
It is to be forbidden that any Christian or gentile ascend to the idol of the capitol for the sake of sacrificing and look. But if he shall have done so, let him be held under the same crime. If he is faithful, after ten years, penance having been performed, let him be received.
¶61
CANON LX
CONCERNING THOSE WHO ARE KILLED WHILE DESTROYING IDOLS
Si quis idola fregerit et ibidem fuerit occisus, quia in evangelio scriptum non est neque invenietur sub apostolis umquam factum, placuit in numerum eum non recipi martyrum.
If anyone shall have broken an idol and been killed there, because it is not written in the Gospel nor will it be found that it was ever done under the apostles, it pleased that he not be received in the number of the martyrs.
¶62
CANON LXI
CONCERNING THOSE WHO ARE JOINED TO TWO SISTERS
Si quis post obitum uxoris suae sororem eius duxerit et ipsa sit fidelis, quinquennio a communione placuit abstineri, nisi forte velocius dari pacem necessitas coegerit infirmitatis.
If anyone after the death of his wife shall have married her sister, and she is faithful, it pleased that he be abstained from communion for five years, unless perhaps the necessity of infirmity compelling, peace be given more quickly.
¶63
CANON LXII
CONCERNING CHARIOTEERS AND PANTOMIMES, IF THEY CONVERT
Si auriga aut pantomimus credere voluerint, placuit, ut prius artibus suis renuntient et tunc demum suscipiantur, ita ut ulterius non revertantur. Qui, si facere contra interdictum temptaverit, proiciantur ab ecclesia.
If a charioteer or pantomime wishes to believe, it pleased that they first renounce their arts and then at length be received, so that they not return again. If they shall have attempted to act against the interdict, let them be cast out from the church.
¶64
CANON LXIII
CONCERNING WIVES WHO KILL CHILDREN FROM ADULTERY
Si qua mulier per adulterium absente marito conceperit idque post facinus occiderit, placuit ei nec in finem dandam esse communionem, eo quod geminaverit scelus.
If any woman, her husband being absent, shall have conceived through adultery and after the deed shall have killed it, it pleased that communion not be given to her even at the end, because she has doubled the crime.
¶65
CANON LXIV
CONCERNING WOMEN WHO COMMIT ADULTERY WITH STRANGE MEN UNTIL DEATH
Si qua mulier usque in finem mortis suae cum alieno viro fuerit mulier moechata, placuit nec in finem dandam ei esse communionem. Si vero eam reliquerit, post decem annos recipi ad communionem acta legitima poenitentia.
If any woman until the end of her life shall have committed adultery with a strange man, it pleased that communion not be given to her even at the end. But if she shall have left him, after ten years, legitimate penance having been performed, let her be received to communion.
¶66
CANON LXV
CONCERNING THE ADULTEROUS WIVES OF CLERICS
Si cuius clerici uxor fuerit moechata et sciat eam maritus suus moechari et eam non statim proiecerit, nec in finem accipiat communionem, ne ab his, qui exemplum bonae conversationis esse debent, videantur scelerum magisteria procedere.
If the wife of any cleric shall have committed adultery and her husband knows that she commits adultery and does not cast her out immediately, let him not receive communion even at the end, lest from those who ought to be examples of good conversation the teachings of crimes seem to proceed.
¶67
CANON LXVI
CONCERNING THOSE WHO MARRY THEIR STEPDAUGHTERS
Si quis privignam suam duxerit uxorem, eo quod sit incestus, placuit nec in finem dandam esse communionem.
If anyone shall have taken his stepdaughter as wife, because it is incest, it pleased that communion not be given even at the end.
¶68
CANON LXVII
CONCERNING THE MARRIAGE OF A CATECHUMEN WOMAN
Prohibendum, ne qua fidelis vel caticumina aut comicos aut viros scenicos habeant. Quaecumque hoc fecerit, a communione arceatur.
It is to be forbidden that any faithful or catechumen woman have actors or actors for husbands. Whosoever shall have done this, let him be kept away from communion.
¶69
CANON LXVIII
CONCERNING AN ADULTEROUS CATECHUMEN WOMAN WHO KILLS HER CHILD
Caticumina, si per adulterium conceperit, et conceptum necaverit, placuit eam in fine baptizari.
If a catechumen woman has conceived through adultery and has killed the conceived child, it pleased that she be baptized at the end.
¶70
CANON LXIX
CONCERNING MARRIED MEN LATER LAPSED INTO ADULTERY
Si quis forte habens uxorem semel fuerit lapsus, placuit eum quinquennium agere debere de ea re poenitentiam et sic reconciliari, nisi necessitas infirmitatis coegerit ante tempus dare communionem, hoc et circa feminas observandum.
If anyone having a wife shall have once been lapsed, it pleased that he do penance for five years concerning that matter and thus be reconciled, unless the necessity of infirmity compels that communion be given before the time. This is to be observed also concerning women.
¶71
CANON LXX
CONCERNING WOMEN WHO COMMIT ADULTERY WITH THE KNOWLEDGE OF THEIR HUSBANDS
Si conscio marito uxor fuerit moechata, placuit nec in fine dandam ei esse communionem. Si vero eam reliquerit, post decem annos accipiat communionem, si eam, cum scierit adulteram, aliquo tempore in domo sua retinuit.
If, with the husband knowing, the wife has committed adultery, it pleased that communion not be given to her even at the end. But if he shall have left her, after ten years let her receive communion, if he, when he knew her to be adulterous, kept her in his house for some time.
¶72
CANON LXXI
CONCERNING CORRUPTERS OF BOYS
Stupratoribus puerorum nec in finem dandam esse communionem.
It pleased that communion not be given even at the end to corrupters of boys.
¶73
CANON LXXII
CONCERNING ADULTEROUS WIDOWS, IF THEY HAVE AFTERWARDS MARRIED THE SAME
Si qua vidua fuerit moechata et eundem postea habuerit maritum, post quinquennii tempus acta legitima poenitentia placuit eam communionem reconciliari. Si alium duxerit relicto illo, nec in fine dandam ei esse communionem, vel si fuerit ille fidelis, quem accepit, communionem non accipiat, nisi post decem annos acta legitima poenitentia, nisi infirmitas coegerit, velocius dare communionem.
If any widow has committed adultery and afterwards has had the same man as husband, after a space of five years, legitimate penance having been performed, it pleased that she be reconciled to communion. If she shall have taken another, leaving him, let communion not be given to her even at the end; or if he whom she has taken is faithful, let him not receive communion until after ten years, legitimate penance having been performed, unless infirmity compels that communion be given more quickly.
¶74
CANON LXXIII
CONCERNING INFORMERS
Delator, si quis exstiterit fidelis et per delationem eius aliquis fuerit praescriptus vel interfectus, placuit eum nec in fine accipere communionem. Si levior causa fuerit, intra quinquennium accipere poterit communionem. Si caticuminus fuerit, post quinquennii tempora admittatur ad baptismum.
If any faithful person shall have been an informer, and by his information anyone has been proscribed or killed, it pleased that he not receive communion even at the end. If the cause has been lighter, he may receive communion within five years. If he has been a catechumen, after the space of five years let him be admitted to baptism.
¶75
CANON LXXIV
CONCERNING FALSE WITNESSES
Falsus testis, prout crimen est, abstinebitur. Si tamen non fuerit mortis, quod obiecit, et probaverit, quod diu tacuerit, biennii tempore abstinebitur. Si autem non probaverit, conventu clericorum placuit per quinquennium abstineri.
A false witness, according to the crime, shall be abstained. Yet if it has not been a capital matter which he objected, and he has proved what he had long kept silent, he shall be abstained for the space of two years. But if he has not proved it, it pleased that he be abstained for five years after the assembly of the clerics.
¶76
CANON LXXV
CONCERNING THOSE WHO ACCUSE PRIESTS AND DO NOT PROVE IT
Si quis autem episcopum vel presbyterum vel diaconum falsis criminibus appetierit et probare non potuerit, nec in finem dandam ei esse communionem.
If anyone shall have attacked a bishop or presbyter or deacon with false crimes and has not been able to prove it, it pleased that communion not be given to him even at the end.
¶77
CANON LXXVI
CONCERNING DEACONS, IF THEY ARE PROVED TO HAVE SINNED BEFORE THEIR HONOR
Si quis diaconum se permiserit ordinari et postea fuerit detectus in crimine mortis, quod aliquando commiserit, si sponte fuerit confessus, placuit eum acta legitima poenitentia post triennium accipere communionem. Quod si alius eum detexerit, post quinquennium acta poenitentia accipere communionem laicam debere.
If anyone has allowed himself to be ordained a deacon and afterwards has been detected in a capital crime which he once committed, if he has been voluntarily confessed, it pleased that after three years, legitimate penance having been performed, he receive communion. But if another shall have detected him, after five years, penance having been performed, he ought to receive lay communion.
¶78
CANON LXXVII
CONCERNING THE BAPTIZED WHO DIE BEFORE BEING CONFIRMED
Si quis diaconus regens plebem sine episcopo vel presbytero aliquos baptizaverit, episcopus eos per benedictionem perficere debebit. Quod si ante de saeculo recesserit, sub fide, qua quis credidit, poterit esse iustus.
If any deacon, ruling a people without a bishop or presbyter, shall have baptized some, the bishop ought to perfect them by benediction. But if they shall have departed from the world before, under the faith by which he believed, he will be able to be just.
¶79
CANON LXXVIII
CONCERNING MARRIED FAITHFUL, IF THEY HAVE COMMITTED FORNICATION WITH A JEWESS OR A GENTILE WOMAN
Si quis fidelis habens uxorem cum Iudaea vel gentile fuerit moechatus, a communione arceatur. Quod si alius eum detexerit, post quinquennium acta legitima poenitentia poterit dominicae sociari communioni.
If any faithful person having a wife shall have committed fornication with a Jewess or a gentile woman, let him be kept away from communion. But if another shall have detected him, after five years, legitimate penance having been performed, he may be joined to the Lord's communion.
¶80
CANON LXXIX
CONCERNING THOSE WHO PLAY AT DICE
Si quis fidelis alea, id est tabula, luserit, placuit eum abstineri, et si emendatus cessaverit, post annum poterit communioni reconciliari.
If any faithful person has played at dice, that is, at the board, it pleased that he be abstained, and if, corrected, he shall have ceased, after a year he may be reconciled to communion.
¶81
CANON LXXX
CONCERNING FREEDMEN
Prohibendum, ut liberti, quorum patroni in saeculo fuerint, ad clerum non prohibeantur.
It is to be forbidden that freedmen, whose patrons have been in the world, be not prohibited from the clergy.
¶82
CANON LXXXI
CONCERNING LETTERS OF WOMEN
Ne feminae suo potius absque maritorum nominibus laicis scribere audeant, qui fideles sunt, vel litteras alicuius pacificas ad suum solum nomen scriptas accipiant.
That women not presume to write letters to lay faithful in their own name rather without the names of their husbands, nor receive letters of peace from anyone written to their sole name.
¶83

This completes the Council of Elvira (Concilium Eliberritanum c. A.D. 300–306) – all 81 canons.

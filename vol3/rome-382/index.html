Roman Synod under Damasus

Concilium Romanum

382

Nunc vero de scripturis divinis agendum est, quid universalis catholica recipiat ecclesia et quid vitare debeat.
Now it must be spoken concerning the divine Scriptures, what the universal Catholic Church receives and what it ought to avoid.
¶1
Incipit ordo veteris testamenti. Genesis liber unus, Exodus liber unus, Leviticus liber unus, Numeri liber unus, Deuteronomium liber unus, Iesus Naue liber unus, Iudicum liber unus, Ruth liber unus, Regnorum libri quattuor, Paralipomenon libri duo, Psalmi centum quinquaginta liber unus, Salomonis libri tres: Proverbiorum liber unus, Ecclesiastes liber unus, Canticum Canticorum liber unus; item Sapientiae liber unus, Ecclesiasticus liber unus.
It begins with the order of the Old Testament. Genesis, one book; Exodus, one book; Leviticus, one book; Numbers, one book; Deuteronomy, one book; Joshua [son of] Nun, one book; Judges, one book; Ruth, one book; Kings, four books; Paralipomenon, two books; One hundred and fifty Psalms, one book; Solomon, three books: Proverbs, one book, Ecclesiastes, one book, Song of Songs, one book; again, Wisdom, one book; Ecclesiasticus, one book.
¶2
Item ordo prophetarum. Isaias liber unus, Hieremias liber unus cum Cinoth id est lamentationibus suis, Ezechiel liber unus, Danihel liber unus, Osee liber unus, Amos liber unus, Micheas liber unus, Iohel liber unus, Abdias liber unus, Ionas liber unus, Naum liber unus, Habacuc liber unus, Sophonias liber unus, Aggeus liber unus, Zacharias liber unus, Malachias liber unus.
Then the order of the prophets. Isaiah, one book; Jeremiah, one book, with Cinoth, that is, his Lamentations; Ezekiel, one book; Daniel, one book; Hosea, one book; Amos, one book; Micah, one book; Joel, one book; Obadiah, one book; Jonah, one book; Nahum, one book; Habakkuk, one book; Zephaniah, one book; Haggai, one book; Zechariah, one book; Malachi, one book.
¶3
Item ordo historiarum. Iob liber unus, Thobias liber unus, Hesdrae libri duo, Hester liber unus, Iudith liber unus, Machabaeorum libri duo.
Then the order of the histories. Job, one book; Tobit, one book; Ezra, two books; Esther, one book; Judith, one book; Maccabees, two books.
¶4
Item ordo scripturarum novi et aeterni testamenti, quod sancta et catholica recipit ecclesia. Evangelia: secundum Matthaeum liber unus, secundum Marcum liber unus, secundum Lucam liber unus, secundum Iohannem liber unus.
Then the order of the Scriptures of the New and eternal Testament, which the holy and Catholic Church receives. Gospels: one book according to Matthew, one book according to Mark, one book according to Luke, one book according to John.
¶5
Epistulae Pauli apostoli numero quattuordecim: ad Romanos una, ad Corinthios duae, ad Ephesios una, ad Thessalonicenses duae, ad Galatas una, ad Philippenses una, ad Colossenses una, ad Timotheum duae, ad Titum una, ad Philemonem una, ad Hebraeos una.
The epistles of Paul, in number fourteen: one to the Romans; two to the Corinthians; one to the Ephesians; two to the Thessalonians; one to the Galatians; one to the Philippians; one to the Colossians; two to Timothy; one to Titus; one to Philemon; one to the Hebrews.
¶6
Item Apocalypsis Iohannis liber unus.
Then the Apocalypse of John, one book.
¶7
Et Acta Apostolorum liber unus.
And the Acts of the Apostles, one book.
¶8
Item canonicae epistulae numero septem: Petri apostoli epistulae duae, Iacobi apostoli epistula una, Iohannis apostoli epistula una, alterius Iohannis presbyteri epistulae duae, Iudae zelotis apostoli epistula una.
Then the canonical epistles, in number seven: two epistles of the Apostle Peter, one epistle of the Apostle James, one epistle of the Apostle John, two epistles of the other John the presbyter, one epistle of the Apostle Jude the Zealot.
¶9
Explicit canon novi testamenti.
This is the canon of the New Testament.
¶10

This completes the Decretum Damasi (Canon of Scripture) from the Council of Rome (382).

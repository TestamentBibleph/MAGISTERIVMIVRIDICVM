Second Council of Carthage

Concilium Carthaginense II

c. 390

INCIPIUNT GESTA CONCILII CARTAGINENSIS SECUNDI HABITA ERA CCCCXXXIII
I Ut trinitas et credatur et praedicetur.
I That the Trinity both be believed and be preached.
II Ut castitas a levitis et sacerdotibus custodiatur.
II That chastity by levites and priests be guarded.
III Ut chrisma et benedictio puellarum et reconciliatio poenitentium a presbyteris non fiat.
III That chrism and the blessing of girls and the reconciliation of penitents by presbyters not be done.
IIII Ut presbyter poenitentem iussus ab episcopo suo reconciliet.
IIII That a presbyter, having been commanded by his bishop, reconcile a penitent.
V Ut diocesis, quae episcopum numquam habuit, non habeat.
V That a diocese which has never had a bishop, let it not have [one].
VI Ut qui criminibus implicati sunt, clericos non accusent.
VI That those who have been implicated in crimes, not accuse clerics.
VII Ut qui excommunicatum alterius susceperit, excommunicetur.
VII That whoever shall have received an excommunicated person of another, let him be excommunicated.
VIII Ut si excommunicatus presbyter sacrificare praesumpserit, anathematizetur.
VIII That if an excommunicated presbyter shall have presumed to sacrifice, let him be anathematized.
VIIII That if a presbyter without consulting the bishop shall have celebrated the rites, let him be deprived of honor.
¶1
VIIII Ut si presbyter inconsulto episcopo agenda celebraverit, honore privetur.
X Quanti debeant iudicare sacerdotem atque levitam.
X How many ought to judge a bishop and a levite.
XI Ut nullus parrochiam alienam praesumat.
XI That no one presume upon another's parish.
XII Ut sine consulto metropolitani nullus ordinetur episcopus.
XII That without the consultation of the metropolitan, no bishop be ordained.
XIII Ut qui contra professionem vel subscriptionem suam venerit, in concilio deponatur.
XIII That whoever against his profession or subscription shall have come, in the council let him be deposed.
Gloriosissimo imperatore Valentiniano Augusto IIII et Theodosio III, viris clarissimis consulibus, III KL. Iunii Carthagine in basilica Perpetua restituta, cum Geneclius episcopus una cum Victore Abdiritano et alio Victore Puppianense et ceteris coepiscopis suis provinciarum diversarum consedisset,
With the most glorious emperor Valentinianus Augustus for the fourth time and Theodosius for the third time, the most renowned men, as consuls, on the third day before the Kalends of June at Carthage in the Basilica of Perpetua restored, when Geneclius the bishop together with Victor of Abdirita and another Victor of Puppiana and with his other fellow bishops of diverse provinces had sat down,
¶2
I Geneclius episcopus dixit: Omnipotenti deo gratias refero, qui permisit, ut secundum commune consilium habitum in praetorio etiam ita tenorem petitionis litterarum mearum ad Carthaginem pro fide atque humilitate ecclesiastica veniretis, licet non omnes tamen, quoniam totum caritas persuadet, ut qui corpore sunt absentes, nobiscum spiritu videantur esse coniuncti, idcirco deo propitio, quia praesentes estis pari professione fides ecclesiastica, quae per nos traditur, in hoc coetu glorioso primitus confitenda est, unde nunc ordo ecclesiasticus singulorum ex consensu est exstruendus atque roborandus, ut fratrum et coepiscoporum nuper promotorum omnium mentes de his, quae proponenda sunt, roborentur, et sicut a patribus certa dispositione accepimus, ut trinitatem, quam nostris in sinibus consecratam retinemus, patris et filii et spiritus sancti unitatem, quae nullam noscitur habere differentiam, sicut didicimus, dei populos instruamus. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Plane sic accipimus, sic tenemus fidem apostolicam sequentes.
I Bishop Geneclius said: To almighty God I give thanks, who has permitted that according to the common counsel held in the praetorium, even thus the tenor of the petition of my letters to Carthage on behalf of faith and ecclesiastical humility you might come, although not all, yet because charity persuades the whole, that those who are absent in body, with us in spirit be seen to be joined. Therefore by God propitious, because you are present with equal profession, the ecclesiastical faith which through us is handed down, in this glorious assembly first must be confessed, from which now the ecclesiastical order of each one from consent is to be built up and strengthened, so that the minds of brothers and fellow bishops recently promoted of all concerning those things which are to be proposed may be strengthened, and just as from the fathers by certain disposition we have received, that the Trinity which in our bosoms consecrated we retain, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit the unity, which no difference is known to have, just as we have learned, the peoples of God we may instruct. By all the bishops it was said: Entirely thus we receive, thus we hold, the apostolic faith following.
¶3
DE CONTINENTIA
II Aurelius episcopus dixit: Cum in praeterito concilio de continentiae et castitatis moderamine tractaretur, gradus isti tres conscriptione quadam castitatis per consecrationes annex sunt: episcopos, inquam, presbyteros et diaconos ita placuit, ut condecet, sacrosanctos antistites ac dei sacerdotes necnon et levitas, vel qui sacramentis divinis inserviunt, continentes esse in omnibus, quo possint simpliciter, quod a deo postulant, impetrare, ut, quod apostoli docuerunt et ipsa servavit antiquitas, nos quoque custodiamus. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Omnibus placet, ut episcopi, presbyteri et diaconi, vel qui sacramenta contrectant, pudicitiae custodes etiam ab uxoribus se abstineant. Ab omnibus dictum est: Placet, ut in omnibus et ab omnibus pudicitia custodiatur, qui altario inserviunt.
II Bishop Aurelius said: Since in the former council concerning the moderation of continence and chastity it was treated, these three grades by a certain inscription of chastity through consecrations have been annexed. Bishops, I say, presbyters and deacons, thus it pleased, just as it befits, that sacrosanct prelates and priests of God and also levites, or those who serve the divine sacraments, be continent in all things, so that they might be able simply what from God they ask to obtain, so that what the apostles taught and antiquity itself guarded, we also may guard. By all the bishops it was said: It pleases all, that bishops, presbyters and deacons, or those who handle the sacraments, as guardians of chastity, even from wives they abstain. By all it was said: It pleases, that in all things and by all chastity be guarded by those who serve the altar.
¶4
UT CASTITAS A LEVITIS ET SACERDOTIBUS CUSTODIATUR
II Epigonius episcopus vullensium regionum dixit: Cum preterito concilio de continentia et castitate tractaretur, gradus isti tres, qui contritione quadam castitatis pro consecratione adnecti sunt, episcopus, inquam, presbyter et diaconus, tractatu pleniori, ut pudicitiam custodiant, doceantur. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Ut superius dictum est, decet sacrosanctos antestites ac dei sacerdotes necnon levitas vel qui sacramentis divinis inserviunt continentes esse in omnibus, quo possint simpliciter, quod a domino postulant, impetrare, ut, quod apostoli docuerunt, et ipsa servavit antiquitas, nunc quoque custodiamus. Ab universis episcopis, presbiteris et diaconis pudicitiae custodiatur, set etiam ab uxoribus se abstineant, ut ab omnibus pudicitia custodiatur, qui altario inserviunt.
II Bishop Epigonius of the Vullensian regions said: Since in the former council concerning continence and chastity it was treated, these three grades, which by a certain contrition of chastity for consecration are annexed, the bishop, I say, presbyter and deacon, by a fuller treatment, that chastity they guard, let them be taught. Bishop Geneclius said: As above has been said, it befits sacrosanct prelates and priests of God and also levites or those who serve the divine sacraments to be continent in all things, so that they might be able simply what from the Lord they ask to obtain, so that what the apostles taught and antiquity itself guarded, now also we may guard. By all the bishops, presbyters and deacons, let chastity be guarded, but also from wives let them abstain, so that by all chastity be guarded by those who serve the altar.
¶5
UT CHRISMA ET BENEDICTIO PUELLARUM ET RECONCILIATIO POENITENTIUM A PRESBYTERIS NON FIAT
III Fortunatus episcopus dixit: Si iubet sanctitas vestra, suggero, nam memini praeterito concilio fuisse statutum, ut chrisma vel reconciliatio poenitentium necnon et puellarum consecratio a presbyteris non fiat. Si quis autem emerserit hoc faciens, quid de eo statuendum sit? Aurelius episcopus dixit: Audivit dignatio vestra suggestionem fratris et coepiscopi nostri Fortunati, quid ad haec dicitis? Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Chrismatis confectio et puellarum consecratio a presbyteris non fiat, vel reconciliare quemquam in publica missa presbytero non licere, hoc omnibus placet.
III Bishop Fortunatus said: If your sanctity commands, I suggest, for I remember that in the former council it was established, that chrism or reconciliation of penitents and also the consecration of girls by presbyters not be done. But if anyone shall have emerged doing this, what concerning him must be established? Bishop Aurelius said: Your dignity has heard the suggestion of our brother and fellow bishop Fortunatus. What to these things do you say? By all the bishops it was said: The preparation of chrism and the consecration of girls by presbyters not be done, nor to reconcile anyone in the public mass for a presbyter be it permitted. This pleases all.
¶6
DE HIS, QUI IN PERICULO POSITI RECONCILIANTUR
IIII Aurelius episcopus dixit: Si quisquam in periculo fuerit constitutus et se reconciliari divinis altaribus petierit, si episcopus absens fuerit, debet utique presbyter consulere episcopum, et sic periclitantem eius praecepto reconciliare, quam rem debemus salubri consilio roborare. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Placet, quod sanctitas vestra necessario nos instruere dignata est.
IIII Bishop Aurelius said: If anyone shall have been placed in peril and shall have sought himself to be reconciled to the divine altars, if the bishop shall be absent, the presbyter ought surely to consult the bishop, and thus the one in peril by his command to reconcile, which matter we ought by salutary counsel to strengthen. By all the bishops it was said: It pleases, what your sanctity has deemed worthy to instruct us necessarily.
¶7
DE HIS, QUI IN PERICULO POSITI RECONCILIANTUR
V Felix episcopus Selemselitanus dixit: Etiam si hoc placet sanctitati vestrae, insinuo, ut dioceses, quae numquam episcopos acceperunt, non habeant, et illa diocesis, quae aliquando habuit, habeat proprium. Et si accedente tempore, crescente fide dei populus multiplicatus desideraverit proprium habere rectorem, eius videlicet voluntate, in cuius potestate est diocesis constituta, habeat episcopum. Secundum autem hanc prosecutionem sanctitas vestra aestimet, quid fieri debeat. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Si placet, insinuatio fratris et coepiscopi nostri Felicis ab omnibus confirmetur. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Placet.
V Bishop Felix of Selemselita said: Even if this pleases your sanctity, I make known, that dioceses which have never received bishops, let them not have [them], and that diocese which once had [one], let it have its own. And if with time advancing, with the faith of God increasing, the people having been multiplied shall have desired to have its own rector, by the will clearly of him in whose power the diocese is constituted, let it have a bishop. According moreover to this prosecution, let your sanctity judge what must be done. Bishop Geneclius said: If it pleases, the insinuation of our brother and fellow bishop Felix by all be confirmed. By all the bishops it was said: It pleases.
¶8
DE HIS, QUI IN ACCUSATIONEM MAIORIS NATU VENIUNT, ET UT EPISCOPUM NULLI CRIMINOSO LICEAT ACCUSARE
VI Numidius episcopus Massilitanus dixit: Praeterea sunt quamplurimi non bonae conversationis, qui existimant maiores natu vel episcopos passim vageque in accusationem pulsandos: debent tam facile admitti necne? Aurelius episcopus dixit: Placet igitur caritati vestrae, ut is, qui aliquibus sceleribus irretitus est, vocem adversus maiorem natu non habeat accusandi? Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Si criminosus est, non admittatur, omnibus placet.
VI Bishop Numidius of Maxilita said: Moreover there are very many of not good conversation, who think that older men or bishops promiscuously and vaguely into accusation must be summoned. Ought they so easily to be admitted or not? Bishop Aurelius said: Does it please therefore your charity that he who has been ensnared by any crimes, a voice against an older man he not have for accusing? By all the bishops it was said: If he is criminal, let him not be admitted. It pleases all.
¶9
DE HIS, QUI MERITO FACINORUM SUORUM AB ECCLESIAE CONGREGATIONE PELLUNTUR
VII Felix episcopus Selemselitanus dixit: Illud autem vestrae suggero sanctitati, ut hi, qui pro facinoribus suis de ecclesia pelluntur et ausi fuerint aut ad comitatum pergere aut ad iudicia publica prosilire, aut si forsitan ecclesiae catholicae limina conturbare episcopus vel clericus cuiuslibet plebis eos sine consensu susceperit, de his, quid censetis? Epigonius episcopus Bullensium regionum dixit: Si quis episcopus communionem tenens catholicam huiusmodi homines vanis blandimentis incedentes temere susceperit, sciat cum his reiectis esse depravatum vel damnatum. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Ergo recte suggerunt fratres et coepiscopi nostri, ut, qui merito facinorum suorum ab ecclesia pulsi sunt, si ab aliquo episcopo aut presbytero vel clerico fuerint in communione suscepti, etiam ipse pari cum eisdem crimine teneatur obnoxius, refugientes sui episcopi regulare iudicium? Ab universis dictum est: Omnibus placet.
VII Bishop Felix of Selemselita said: Moreover this I suggest to your sanctity, that those who on account of their crimes from the church are driven out and shall have dared either to go to the court or to rush to the public judgments, or if perhaps the thresholds of the catholic church to disturb a bishop or cleric of any people without consent shall have received them, concerning these, what do you decree? Bishop Epigonius of the Bullensian regions said: If any bishop holding catholic communion such men with vain blandishments advancing rashly shall have received, let him know with those rejected to be depraved or damned. Bishop Geneclius said: Therefore rightly suggest our brothers and fellow bishops, that those who by merit of their crimes from the church have been driven out, if by any bishop or presbyter or cleric they shall have been received into communion, even he himself by the same crime be held bound. Those fleeing the regular judgment of their bishop? By all it was said: It pleases all.
¶10
DE PRESBYTERIS, QUI A PROPRIIS CORRIPIUNTUR EPISCOPIS
VIII Alipius episcopus ecclesiae Tagastensis legatus provinciae Numidiae dixit: Nec illud praetermittendum est, ut, si quis forte presbyter ab episcopo suo correptus aut excommunicatus, tumore vel superbia inflatus, putaverit separatim se deo sacrificia offerenda vel aliud erigendum altare contra ecclesiasticam fidem disciplinamque crediderit agendum, non exeat impunitus. Valentinus primae sedis provinciae Numidiae dixit: Necessario disciplinae ecclesiasticae et fidei congrua sunt, quae frater noster Alipius prosecutus est. Proinde, quid exinde videtur vestrae dilectioni, edicite. Si quis presbyter contra episcopum suum inflatus scisma fecerit, anathema sit. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Si quis presbyter a praeposito suo excommunicatus vel correptus fuerit, debet utique apud vicinos episcopos conquaeri, ut ab ipsis eius causa possit audiri ac per ipsos suo episcopo reconciliari. Quod, nisi fecerit, sed superbia, quod absit, inflatus secernendum se ab episcopi sui communione duxerit ac separatim cum aliquibus scisma faciens sacrificium deo obtulerit, loco amisso anathema habeatur et locum amittat. Nihilominus et de civitate et congregatione, in qua fuerit, longius repellatur, ne vel ignorantes vel simpliciter viventes serpentina fraude decipiat, quoniam secundum apostolum ecclesia una est, una fides, unum baptisma. Et si querimoniam iustam adversus episcopum non habuerit, inquirendum erit.
VIII Bishop Alipius of the church of Tagasta, legate of the province of Numidia, said: Nor that ought to be omitted, that if anyone perhaps a presbyter by his bishop having been corrected or excommunicated, with swelling or pride inflated, shall have thought separately to himself sacrifice to be offered or another altar to be erected against ecclesiastical faith and discipline believed to be done, let him not go unpunished. Valentinus of the first see of the province of Numidia said: Necessarily to ecclesiastical discipline and to faith congruent are those things which our brother Alipius has set forth. Therefore, what concerning that seems to your love, declare. If any presbyter against his bishop inflated a schism shall have made, let him be anathema. By all the bishops it was said: If any presbyter by his superior excommunicated or corrected shall have been, he ought surely among the neighboring bishops to complain, so that by them his cause may be heard and through them to his bishop be reconciled. Which, if he shall not have done, but by pride, which may it be absent, inflated, himself from his bishop's communion he shall have thought to separate and separately with some making schism a sacrifice to God shall have offered, having lost his place, let him be held anathema and his place let him lose. Nevertheless also from the city and congregation in which he shall have been, let him be driven far away, lest either the ignorant or those living simply with serpentine fraud he may deceive, since according to the apostle, the church is one, one faith, one baptism. And if a just complaint against his bishop he shall not have, it must be inquired.
¶11
DE PRESBYTERIS, QUI A PROPRIIS CORRIPIUNTUR EPISCOPIS
VIIII Numidius episcopus Maxilitanus dixit: In quibusdam locis sunt presbyteri, qui aut ignorantes simpliciter aut dissimulantes audaciter praesente et inconsulto episcopo cum plurimis in domiciliis agant agenda, quod disciplinae et incongruum esse cognoscit sanctitas vestra. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Fratris et coepiscopi nostri dignae satisfactioni respondere non moremur. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Quisquis presbyter inconsulto episcopo agendam in quolibet loco voluerit celebrare, ipse honori suo contrarius exsistit.
VIIII Bishop Numidius of Maxilita said: In certain places there are presbyters who either ignorantly simply or dissemblingly audaciously, the bishop present and not consulted, with very many in private houses conduct the rites, which to discipline and incongruous to be your sanctity knows. Bishop Geneclius said: To the worthy satisfaction of our brother and fellow bishop to respond we do not delay. By all the bishops it was said: Whoever presbyter, not having consulted the bishop, the rites in any place shall have wished to celebrate, he himself to his own honor contrary exists.
¶12
SI QUIS EPISCOPUS ABSQUE TEMPORE SYNODI IN CRIMINE DETRACTUS FUERIT, A DUODECIM EPISCOPIS AUDIATUR
X Felix episcopus Selemselitanus dixit: Etiam hoc adicio secundum statuta veterum conciliorum, ut si quis episcopus, quod non optamus, in reatum aliquem incurrerit et fuerit ei nimia necessitas non posse plurimos congregare, ne in crimine remaneat, a duodecim episcopis audiatur et a sex presbyter et a tribus diaconus cum proprio suo episcopo. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Quid ad haec dicit sanctitas vestra? Ab universis episcopis dictum est: A nobis veterum statuta debere servari.
X Bishop Felix of Selemselita said: Even this I add according to the statutes of ancient councils, that if any bishop, which we do not wish, into any fault shall have incurred and there shall have been for him too great a necessity not to be able to gather very many, lest in crime he remain, by twelve bishops let him be heard, and a presbyter by six, and a deacon by three, with his own bishop. Bishop Geneclius said: What to these things does your sanctity say? By all the bishops it was said: By us the statutes of the ancients ought to be guarded.
¶13
SI QUIS EPISCOPUS ABSQUE TEMPORE SYNODI IN CRIMINE DETRACTUS FUERIT, A DUODECIM EPISCOPIS AUDIATUR
XI Victor episcopus Abziritanus dixit: Roboranda est ecclesiastica disciplina, ne quisquam episcoporum alterius plebes vel diocesim sua importunitate pulsare debeat. Qui hoc facere temptaverit, quemadmodum coercendus est? Geneclius episcopus dixit: Et lex sancta prohibet, et ipsa veritas definit non debere quemquam aliena concupiscere. Proinde, quid etiam vobis omnibus placeat, propria voce signate. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Placet et secundum divinae legis et sancti evangelii auctoritatem, ut nemo nostrum alienos limites transgrediatur.
XI Bishop Victor of Abzirita said: To be strengthened is ecclesiastical discipline, lest any bishop another's peoples or diocese by his own importunity to disturb ought. He who this shall have attempted, how is he to be coerced? Bishop Geneclius said: Both the holy law prohibits, and truth itself defines that no one ought to covet another's things. Therefore, what even to all you pleases, with your own voice signify. By all the bishops it was said: It pleases, and according to the authority of the divine law and the holy gospel, that no one of us another's boundaries transgress.
¶14
XII Numidius episcopus Maxilitanus dixit: Aliqui episcopi usurpatione quadam existimant contempto primate cuiuslibet provinciae sive ad desiderium populi episcopum ordinare sine litteris ad se primae cathedrae manentibus neque postea acceptis. Quid de hoc statuat sanctitas vestra? Geneclius episcopus dixit: Quoniam communis est honorificentia, quae unicuique servari oportet et ipsum fratrem vestrum pronuntiare. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Placet omnibus, ut inconsulto primate cuiuslibet provinciae tam facile nemo praesumat, licet cum multis episcopis in quocumque loco sine eius, ut dictum est, praecepto episcopum ordinare. Si autem necessitas fuerit, tres episcopi, in quocumque loco sint, cum primatis praecepto ordinare debeant episcopum.
XII Bishop Numidius of Maxilita said: Some bishops by a certain usurpation think, the primate of any province having been despised, either at the desire of the people a bishop to ordain without letters to them from the first see remaining nor afterwards received. What concerning this does your sanctity establish? Bishop Geneclius said: Since common is the honorificence which to each one to be guarded befits, also that same brother your judgment to pronounce. By all the bishops it was said: It pleases all, that without consulting the primate of any province so easily no one presume, although with many bishops in any place without his, as it has been said, command a bishop to ordain. But if necessity shall have been, three bishops, in whatever place they may be, with the command of the primate ought to ordain a bishop.
¶15
XIII Geneclius episcopus dixit: Omnia ergo, quae a coetu gloriosissimo statuta sunt, placet ab omnibus custodiri? Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Placet, ut custodiantur ab omnibus. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Si, quod non opinamur, ab aliquo fuerint violata, quid statuitis, quid fieri debeat? Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Qui contra suam professionem vel subscriptionem venerit, ipse se ab hoc coetu separabit. Geneclius episcopus dixit: Gratulemur deo nostro praestante, quod pro statu ecclesiae catholicae cuncta nos salubri consilio servanda decrevimus. Et ideo, quae ab omnibus sunt dicta, propria debemus subscriptione roborari. Ab universis episcopis dictum est: Fiat, fiat.
XIII Bishop Geneclius said: All things therefore which by the most glorious assembly have been established, does it please that by all be guarded? By all the bishops it was said: It pleases, that they be guarded by all. Bishop Geneclius said: If, which we do not suppose, by anyone they shall have been violated, what do you establish, what must be done? By all the bishops it was said: Whoever against his profession or subscription shall have come, he himself from this assembly will separate himself. Bishop Geneclius said: Let us give thanks to our God granting, that for the state of the catholic church all things we have decreed by salutary counsel to be guarded. And therefore, which by all have been spoken, by our own subscription we ought to be strengthened. By all the bishops it was said: Let it be done, let it be done.
¶16
Et subscripserunt.
And they subscribed.
